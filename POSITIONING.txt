MathQuest Positioning Statement:

"An open-source educational game framework for turning mathematics
practice into interactive game experiences, designed to run offline
on modest hardware and extensible toward physical arcade/Arduino controls."

NOT "un juego escolar de matemáticas" — presente como framework extensible,
no solo un juego escolar. Lenguaje realista, sin afirmaciones de impacto
no demostrado. Offline-first, modest hardware, Arduino extensibility.
